### 👋 hey, I'm Rayhan

I design it, build the front end and the back end, and get it in front of real people before it is ready. Most of what I make exists because I wanted it to and got impatient waiting for someone else to build it. I vibecode with Claude Code as my main tool, which is exactly why I can go from a sketch to a shipped app in a weekend. The interesting part was never typing the code, it was knowing what to build and having the taste to tell good from just-looks-good.

```
what I care about   →   ship it, watch it break, fix it. real feedback beats theory.
how I work          →   own it end to end. design, build, sell, repeat.
the bet             →   the future belongs to generalists who can go one level deeper.
```

### 🚀 building right now

- **[Lumi](https://trylumi.co)** is a personal AI tutor that teaches on a live whiteboard, drawing the diagram while it explains it. You can cut in mid-sentence and it re-explains a different way. Built against the exam boards, not a generic syllabus. **140 users in its first 5 days.**
- **Arclight** does applied AI engineering for companies in London and the Gulf, run through a network of builders I meet at hackathons.
